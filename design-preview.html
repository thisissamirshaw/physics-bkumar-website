Method

How every chapter is taught

The same four steps, in the same order, for every topic from kinematics to semiconductors.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Start from what is physically happening, with a diagram on the board before any formula.

  2. 02

    Derivation

    Build the result yourself, so v² = u² + 2as is something you can rebuild, not memorise.

  3. 03

    Problems

    Graded sets, from NCERT to JEE Advanced, solved in class and then on your own.

  4. 04

    Test & review

    A timed chapter test, then a one-on-one review of every mistake.
